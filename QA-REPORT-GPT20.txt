Important platform behaviour
The app sends the image/file and website link together through the browser's native share system. The receiving application (for example WhatsApp, email or Messages) ultimately decides how it displays or retains the supplied text and URL. GPT20 includes the visible Copy website message (backup) option for destinations that remove the caption.
