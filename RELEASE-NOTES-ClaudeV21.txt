Card Maker Messages ClaudeV21

Card layout and workflow corrections.

Card drawing:
- Recipient name now appears on single cards (digital and one-page print),
  reading "To [Name]" as a dedication under the occasion label, matching the
  folded card which already named the recipient inside.
- Name hierarchy corrected. The recipient reads as a clear dedication near the
  top; the sender name is now a small, quiet sign-off at the bottom, sized well
  below the recipient and the headline, the way a real card is signed.
- Decorative motifs no longer cover the sender name. Accents draw behind the
  sign-off, and the corner sprigs on framed and floral designs were moved into
  the corners, clear of the text band, on both mobile and desktop.
- The website line was removed from the back of folded cards. The back is clean
  by default. Any back message the user writes still appears.

Workflow and mobile:
- The stage tabs now stay visible on mobile as a sticky strip above the pinned
  card preview, with completed stages marked, so the current stage is always
  visible and tappable without scrolling back up.
- When the app blocks moving to the next stage before a required step is done,
  the reminder now appears as a banner pinned in view at the top on mobile,
  rather than below the card where it was hidden. It has a dismiss button.

Sharing (optional, off until configured):
- Photo cards can now optionally share as a link with a real preview, uploading
  the finished card from the browser to Cloudinary. Your server never receives
  the photo. Requires a Cloudinary account and two config values. Off by
  default, with the previous direct image share as the fallback. See
  SHARE-PHOTO-SETUP.md. Cards without a photo are unchanged.
- c.php now accepts a hosted image only from res.cloudinary.com and rejects any
  other or malformed URL, falling back to the safe rendered preview.

Cache:
- All asset version queries bumped to v21.
- cmm-build meta and service worker VERSION bumped to 21.

Unchanged:
- All ClaudeV20 functionality: rendering, five-stage workflow, review-screen
  share and save, folded exports, email verification, SMTP, admin dashboard,
  SEO pages, and the og.php preview renderer.
