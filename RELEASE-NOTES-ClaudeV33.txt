Card Maker Messages ClaudeV33

Invisible text fixed correctly, and brand naming on the home page.

The invisible text, properly diagnosed
- My previous fix was wrong and made the problem worse. I had assumed the answer
  paragraph always sat on a dark background, so I forced its text to a light
  colour. The home page hero follows the theme: it is light cream in light mode
  and dark in dark mode. Light text on light cream is invisible, which is exactly
  what you saw.
- The correct fix is that the paragraph now uses only theme tokens for both its
  background and its text, so it flips automatically and correctly with the
  theme on every page. No hardcoded colour is applied to it anywhere that
  follows the theme.
- One section, the tool hero, is dark in both light and dark mode. That is the
  only place with an explicit light text colour, and it is correct there because
  the background is guaranteed dark.

A contrast audit was added so this cannot recur
- Every combination of text and background used by the elements added in recent
  releases was measured in both themes against the WCAG AA standard of 4.5 to 1.
  All twelve combinations pass, the lowest being 6.38 to 1 and the highest
  15.67 to 1. The pre-existing hero paragraph class was also checked in all three
  of its real contexts and passes at 6.21, 9.65 and 11.27 to 1.

Brand naming
- The home page heading is now Card Maker Messages Tool, matching the card maker
  page and the app.
- The home page title and description keep the phrase "card maker", because that
  is what people search for and the branded name has no search volume of its own.
  The brand name is what a visitor reads; the generic term is what search engines
  index.
- The home page answer paragraph opened with a lowercase word, left behind by an
  earlier edit. Fixed, and every page was checked for the same fault.
