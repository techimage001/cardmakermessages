Card Maker Messages ClaudeV32

Contrast fix and product naming.

Invisible text on dark hero sections fixed
- The answer paragraph under the H1 was styled with a light panel background but
  had no explicit text colour, so on the 23 pages where it sits inside the dark
  hero it inherited the hero's light text and became light on light. It was
  effectively invisible.
- Two fixes were applied. The paragraph now sets its text colour explicitly, so
  it can never inherit and disappear anywhere on the site. Separately, inside a
  dark hero it now takes the hero treatment already used elsewhere in the design
  system, a translucent panel with a gold left rule and light text, so it reads
  correctly against the dark background rather than fighting it.
- Every other element added in recent releases was checked for the same fault.
  The footer category links correctly inherit the footer's light colour, and the
  breadcrumb match was a false positive from the scan. The answer paragraph was
  the only genuine case.

Product naming
- The tool is now called Card Maker Messages Tool in the visible headings on the
  card maker page and the app, replacing the generic "Card maker".
- The page titles and meta descriptions deliberately keep the phrase "card
  maker", because that is the term people actually search for and the branded
  name has no search volume of its own. This gives the brand its distinct name
  where a visitor sees it, while keeping the generic term working where search
  engines read it.
