Card Maker Messages ClaudeV26

Three layout corrections.

Category dropdown text:
- The text inside the seven category dropdowns was too large for the column
  width, so "Choose occasion" was being cut off. Reduced so the full wording
  reads clearly in every column at every screen size.

Website address removed from cards:
- The site domain no longer prints on any card, on any panel, in any output
  mode. Previously it was suppressed only on folded card backs.
- The "Show the website address on the back" toggle has been removed from the
  editor, since it no longer does anything, and the dead wiring behind it was
  cleaned out.
- Verified by rendering a card back with the old setting forced on and measuring
  zero ink in the exact area where the domain used to sit.

Spacing above the recipient name:
- The occasion label and the "To [name]" line were too close together. The
  label moved up, the recipient line moved down, and the heading below moved
  down to keep its own clearance.
- Measured on a rendered card, the gap between the two lines has more than
  doubled, from roughly 37 pixels to 81 pixels on a portrait card.

Mobile:
- A separate mobile preview is included in this release, rendered at 390 pixels
  wide, so the phone layout can be checked directly.
- Category columns reflow from seven across to four, then two, then one. Font
  and size controls drop their "Style" label and sit side by side. The footer
  categories reflow to four then two columns. The step strip on the floating
  card collapses to numbers on very narrow screens.

Cache:
- All asset version queries bumped to v26.
- cmm-build meta and service worker VERSION bumped to 26.

Verified before release:
- 14 live DOM checks including the removal of the website toggle and the mobile
  breakpoints.
- Card rendering measured pixel by pixel for both the spacing change and the
  domain removal.
- Both previews boot cleanly with all controls present.

Unchanged:
- All ClaudeV25 functionality: 63 occasions, 7 categories, the category columns,
  the make your own occasion button, per line font and size controls, the
  generated category and occasion pages, and the footer arrangement.
