Questions about ramadan card messages

Should I handwrite the ramadan message?

Printing the message and adding a handwritten line underneath is a good middle ground, and it is what most people do with a printed card. The card maker has a handwritten style among the font choices if you would rather the whole thing looked informal. If you are printing to fold, leave the inside panel lighter than you think you need: handwriting takes more room than typed text, and a cramped inside panel undoes the work the front cover does. How long should a ramadan message be?

One to three sentences suits most ramadan cards. Shorter works better when the relationship is formal or the subject is delicate, because restraint reads as respect. Longer suits someone close who will read the card more than once. The format matters too: a folded card gives you an inside panel with room to write properly, while a card you send digitally reads better short enough to take in at a glance. When you are unsure, write the shorter version and let the specific detail carry the weight.
