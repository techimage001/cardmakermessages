Card Maker Messages ClaudeV27

Mobile layout overhaul and removal of the last duplicated steps.

The steps were listed three times:
- A "How it works" block at the top of the app repeated the same five steps a
  third time, after the wide step bar was removed in V25 and the strip on the
  floating card was kept. That block has been deleted. The five steps now appear
  exactly once, on the floating card.
- On mobile this block was the main reason the category picker sat below the
  fold. Removing it lifts the actual tool into view.

Make Your Own Card Here:
- The button now reads "Make Your Own Card Here" in bold, with the smaller
  supporting line beneath it: "If it is not on the list, type any event and we
  build the card around it."

Mobile layout:
- All five steps now stay visible and labelled on the floating card. The strip
  scrolls sideways rather than dropping the names, so the current stage is always
  readable. Previously the labels were hidden on narrow screens.
- The card preview was taking too much of the screen. Reduced from 42 to 30
  percent of viewport height so the controls beneath are reachable without a long
  scroll.
- The page heading was oversized on phones and pushed everything down. It is now
  a normal heading size, left aligned, and the descriptive paragraph is hidden on
  small screens.
- The seven categories now sit two across on phones and become full width rows on
  very small screens, with the category name and its dropdown side by side so
  every category is visible and selectable without hunting.
- The custom card button, control panel padding and section headings were all
  tightened for phone widths.

Cache:
- All asset version queries bumped to v27.
- cmm-build meta and service worker VERSION bumped to 27.

Verified before release:
- 15 live DOM and stylesheet checks confirming the duplicate step block is gone,
  the steps now appear once, the button wording is correct, and every mobile rule
  is present.
- Desktop and mobile previews included and confirmed booting.

Unchanged:
- All ClaudeV26 functionality: 63 occasions, 7 categories, per line font and size
  controls, generated category and occasion pages, footer arrangement, card
  spacing and the removal of the domain from cards.
