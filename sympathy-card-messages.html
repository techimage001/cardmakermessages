What should I write in a sympathy card?

Keep the message gentle, acknowledge the loss without trying to explain it, and offer specific support. A few sincere sentences are often more comforting than a long message.

  1. Choose the tone and relationship that fit the situation.
  2. Select a message or write your own words.
  3. Personalise the recipient, sender and card design.
  4. Review the finished card before sharing or printing.

How do I turn the message into a card?

Open the card maker with your chosen occasion, add a recipient name and sender, then tap a complete design. Digital cards can be exported as high-resolution images. Printable cards produce a two-page PDF with the outside panels arranged correctly for folding.

  1. Use one of the messages above or write your own.
  2. Choose a one-tap design and optional photo.
  3. Preview the front, inside and back panels.
  4. Open the image or folded PDF before saving it.

Yes. A sympathy and bereavement card can be created as a social image, a one-page printable design or a traditional folded card. Review the final layout before sharing or printing.
