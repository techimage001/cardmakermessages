Questions About New Home Card Messages

Are these new home messages yours to use?

Yes. Everything on this page is written in house and yours to use on any card you make, printed, posted or sent digitally. no watermarks on downloaded cards, no charge. Because the messages are original rather than collected from elsewhere, there is nothing to attribute and no risk of reproducing someone else's copyrighted verse on a card you give away. Use a line word for word, change a few words, or take it purely as a starting point for something of your own. Nothing here is fixed: the new home wording, the design, the size and the font of each line can all be changed before you export the finished card.

How do I make a new home card online?

It takes about five minutes. Pick New Home, choose your wording from this page or write your own, select a size, choose a design, then export. The preview redraws as you go so you are never guessing at the result. If you add a photograph it is processed on your device and never sent to a server, which is worth knowing when the card is going to someone in your family rather than a stranger. If you are still deciding, read the new home examples in two different tones and see which one you would be comfortable reading aloud to the person.

What tone should a new home card use?

Match the tone to the relationship rather than the occasion. This page covers heartfelt, short, funny, formal, religious wording so the choice is deliberate rather than accidental. Warmer, more personal language suits family and close friends. Plainer, more measured language suits colleagues, clients and people you know less well. Faith based wording is included for those who would want it, and can be swapped out entirely if not. You can change tone at any point in the card maker without losing the design work you have already done.

What other cards are similar to new home?

New Home sits in the new beginnings collection alongside New Baby, Pregnancy, Baby Shower. Occasions in the same collection often share a tone, so if the wording here is close but not quite right, a sibling page may have the line you want. If your occasion is not listed anywhere on the site, the card maker has a make your own option where you name the occasion and write every line from scratch, with all the same designs, sizes and export formats available. The new beginnings pages all follow the same structure, so once you have made one new home card the rest take almost no time at all.

Can I add a photo to a new home card?

Yes, and you can reposition and zoom it inside the frame until it sits how you want. The photograph is processed entirely in your browser and never reaches a server, so it stays private to you and whoever receives the card. Because the card is redrawn from raw image data rather than embedding the original file, location information stored inside the photograph does not carry through to the export. You can remove or replace it at any point without losing your wording. Nothing here is fixed: the new home wording, the design, the size and the font of each line can all be changed before you export the finished card.

Can I print a new home card at home?

Yes. Choose a folded size such as A4 folded to A5, or an one page print size, and the card maker exports a print ready PDF with both sheets laid out correctly. Print at actual size or one hundred percent rather than fit to page, or the margins shift and the fold lands in the wrong place. For double sided printing choose landscape orientation and flip on the short edge. Run a test on plain paper first, because printer settings vary and a test sheet costs almost nothing. On the new home specifically, the preview redraws as you change anything, so the result is never a surprise.
