Card Maker Messages ClaudeV35

Create a card buttons now open the right occasion.

The reported bug
- Opening an occasion page such as Valentine's Day and pressing Create a card
  opened the card maker showing a completely different card, in the reported
  case a Confirmation card reading God Bless You.
- The cause was that the hero Create a card button linked to /app.html with no
  occasion attached. With no occasion in the address, the card maker correctly
  resumed whatever card you last worked on, which is the right behaviour for a
  bare link but wrong when arriving from a specific occasion page.
- This is also why the category dropdowns always worked: those set the occasion
  directly in the app rather than travelling through a link.

The fix
- 341 links across 89 pages now carry the occasion, so every occasion page,
  category page, maker page and invitation page opens the card it advertises.
- Category pages open the first occasion in that category. Invitation pages also
  set the creation type, so they open as an invitation rather than a card.
- The three pages that duplicate another page's subject send you to the primary
  occasion, matching where their canonical tag already points.
- 60 links remain deliberately generic: the home page, the card maker page, the
  digital card maker and the legal and guide pages. None of these advertises a
  specific occasion, so resuming your saved card is the correct behaviour there.

Verified before release
- The exact reported scenario was reproduced and then confirmed fixed: with a
  saved Confirmation card in storage, opening the Valentine's Day link now loads
  Happy Valentine's Day rather than God Bless You.
- Confirmed that a bare /app.html link still resumes saved work, since that
  behaviour is correct and had to be preserved.
- Every occasion page was checked programmatically to confirm its button targets
  its own occasion. All pass.
- All 101 pages were compared against the previous release: once the version
  number and the app links are normalised, every page is byte for byte
  identical, so nothing outside this fix changed.
- No broken internal links.
