Card Maker Messages ClaudeV22

Card layout, occasion label, and workflow corrections. Every card-drawing change
in this release was verified by rendering real card images before packaging.

Card drawing:
- The star and sparkle accent no longer sits on the message or the sender name.
  It now sits at the very bottom of the card, is scaled by the smaller card
  dimension, and is hidden on wide cards where there is no room for it.
- Photo cards on wide and landscape sizes no longer overflow the card. The photo
  frame now adapts its height to the card shape instead of assuming a portrait
  card, so the photo, occasion label and text all sit correctly.
- Corner decorations were confirmed clear of the sender sign-off band on the
  framed and floral designs, on both portrait and landscape.

Occasion label:
- The occasion label above the title is now editable. It pre-fills with a
  natural default per occasion (Birthday becomes "Birthday Wishes", and so on)
  and can be changed to anything, for example "Happy 40th" or "Birthday Wishes".
  Capped at 30 characters. When the occasion is switched, a custom label the user
  has typed is kept; the label only auto-updates while it has not been edited.

Workflow:
- A compact stage strip (1 to 5) now sits inside the preview card itself, so the
  current stage travels with the card as it stays in view, with completed stages
  marked. Each is tappable to jump.

Cache:
- All asset version queries bumped to v22.
- cmm-build meta and service worker VERSION bumped to 22.

Unchanged:
- All ClaudeV21 functionality: recipient name on single cards, sender sizing,
  clean folded backs, sticky stage tabs, the pinned guard reminder, the share
  and save options, folded exports, email verification, SMTP, admin dashboard,
  SEO pages, c.php and og.php share previews.
- Sharing is unchanged: no-photo cards share as a link with a preview; photo
  cards share as an image. The optional Cloudinary photo-link path remains off
  unless configured.
