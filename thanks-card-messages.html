Questions about thank you card messages

How long should a thank you message be on a thank you?

There is no fixed length, but thank you messages rarely benefit from going past four sentences. Length is not what makes a card feel meant. Specificity is. A single line naming something real will outlast three paragraphs of general good wishes. If you have chosen a folded card you have an inside panel to fill, so a slightly fuller message sits comfortably there; on a one page or digital card, tighten it. You can switch size at any point without losing what you have already written. Everything here is available on the thank you without a sign up, a watermark or a paid tier.

What tone should a thank you card use with the thank you?

Match the tone to the relationship rather than the occasion. This page covers heartfelt, short, formal, professional, funny, religious, inspirational wording so the choice is deliberate rather than accidental. Warmer, more personal language suits family and close friends. Plainer, more measured language suits colleagues, clients and people you know less well. Can I print a thank you card at home for a thank you?

Home printing works well if you get two settings right. Print at actual size rather than letting the printer scale the page, and when printing both sides choose landscape with short edge flipping so the inside lands the right way up. The exported PDF already has the fold line and panel positions worked out. Card stock behaves differently from paper in most home printers, so it is worth one test sheet before you commit the good stock. Because these thank you lines are written for this site rather than gathered from elsewhere, you can print any of them without worrying about who wrote them first. That holds for the thank you at every size, from a shareable image through to a folded print layout.

What is a short thank you message when making a thank you?

Something like "Thank you so much. Your thoughtfulness was truly appreciated." does the job when space is tight or the relationship is more formal. Short messages are not a lesser option: on a thank you card they often read as more sincere than a long one, because there is nowhere to hide behind phrasing. The rule that helps is to cut anything that would be true of anybody, and keep only what is true of this person. This page has several short options grouped together so you can compare them.
