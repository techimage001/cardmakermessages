Sorry and Apology card messages

Use any of these sorry and apology messages exactly as written, or edit them to sound like you. Each one works on a printed card, a folded card or a digital card you can share.

Heartfelt sorry and apology messages

  • I am truly sorry. I value you and I want to put this right.
  • I have thought about it properly and I understand why it hurt. I am sorry.

Short and simple sorry and apology messages

  • I am sorry. Truly.
  • Please accept my sincere apology.

Formal sorry and apology messages

  • Please accept my sincere apologies for any difficulty this has caused.

Encouraging sorry and apology messages

  • Getting it wrong matters less than what we choose to do next. I want to do better.
