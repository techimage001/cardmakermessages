Questions About Card Messages by Occasion

Does the card messages by occasion work on a phone?

Yes. The layout adapts to phone screens, with the card preview and the step navigation staying in view while you scroll through the controls beneath. You can write, design, preview and export entirely from a phone, including adding a photograph from your camera roll. Printing from a phone depends on your printer app, so for folded cards a laptop is usually easier at the final step. If something does not fit what you need, the wording, design, size and fonts can all be changed before export.

Do I need to sign up before I start for occasions?

None at all. You can begin immediately and only create an account once you have made several cards. Because everything happens locally, your work stays on the device you made it on, which also means clearing your browser data will clear an unfinished card. Export anything you want to keep rather than relying on it being there tomorrow. If something does not fit what you need, the wording, design, size and fonts can all be changed before export. Everything described here applies to the card messages by occasion specifically, not just to the site in general.

Will there be a watermark on my card messages by occasion?

No. Nothing you export carries a watermark, a logo or any reference to this site, at any size or in any format. That includes the PDF export, which on many comparable tools is where branding is added or resolution is reduced. What you download is the card exactly as you designed it, ready to print or send without anything to crop out first. The preview redraws as you work, so you are never guessing at what the exported file will look like. If something does not fit what you need, the wording, design, size and fonts can all be changed before export.

What does the card messages by occasion cost?

Yes, open to everyone, with no paid tier. Every design, size and export format is available from the first visit. You write the wording, choose a design, set the font and size of each line separately, then download a print ready PDF or an image file. There is no limit on how many you make, and features other sites charge for, including high resolution export and removing branding from the finished card, cost nothing here. If something does not fit what you need, the wording, design, size and fonts can all be changed before export. Everything here is available on the occasions without a sign up, a watermark or a paid tier.

Can I change the font and text size with the occasions?

Each line is controlled independently, which is the difference between a template and a design tool. You can make the heading large and the sign off small, or set the message in a different face from the title. The seven typefaces are all standard system fonts, chosen deliberately so that nothing needs downloading and nothing carries a licence restriction onto the cards you make. If something does not fit what you need, the wording, design, size and fonts can all be changed before export.

Can I add my own photo for occasions?

Yes, and you can reposition or zoom it inside the frame until it sits how you want. The image is processed entirely in your browser and never reaches our servers, so it stays private to you and to whoever receives the card. Because the card is redrawn from raw image data rather than embedding the original file, location information stored inside the photograph does not carry through to the export. Everything described here applies to the card messages by occasion specifically, not just to the site in general.

Can I print what I make for occasions?

Yes. Choose a folded size or an one page print size and the tool exports a print ready PDF laid out correctly for home printing. Print at actual size or one hundred percent rather than fit to page, or the margins shift and the fold lands in the wrong place. For double sided printing choose landscape orientation and flip on the short edge. Test on plain paper first, because printer settings vary between machines. The preview redraws as you work, so you are never guessing at what the exported file will look like.
