Questions about welcome back card messages

How long should a welcome back message be?

One to three sentences suits most welcome back cards. Shorter works better when the relationship is formal or the subject is delicate, because restraint reads as respect. Longer suits someone close who will read the card more than once. The format matters too: a folded card gives you an inside panel with room to write properly, while a card you send digitally reads better short enough to take in at a glance. When you are unsure, write the shorter version and let the specific detail carry the weight.

Can I print a welcome back card at home?

Home printing works well if you get two settings right. Print at actual size rather than letting the printer scale the page, and when printing both sides choose landscape with short edge flipping so the inside lands the right way up. The exported PDF already has the fold line and panel positions worked out. Card stock behaves differently from paper in most home printers, so it is worth one test sheet before you commit the good stock. What is a short welcome back message?

Something like "Welcome back." does the job when space is tight or the relationship is more formal. Short messages are not a lesser option: on a welcome back card they often read as more sincere than a long one, because there is nowhere to hide behind phrasing. The rule that helps is to cut anything that would be true of anybody, and keep only what is true of this person. This page has several short options grouped together so you can compare them. If you are still deciding, read the welcome back examples in two different tones and see which one you would be comfortable reading aloud to the person.

What do you write in a welcome back card?

Name one specific thing rather than reaching for a general sentiment. On a welcome back card that might be something the person did, something you noticed, or what you hope comes next for them. Two sincere sentences almost always read better than a paragraph of filler. There are 6 examples on this page across 5 tones, so you can find a line that matches your relationship and use it directly. If none fits exactly, take the closest one and change the details until it sounds like something you would actually say out loud.
