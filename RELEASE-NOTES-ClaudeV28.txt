Card Maker Messages ClaudeV28

Wording, step strip proportions and mobile floating card.

Make Your Own Card Here:
- The supporting line no longer claims the site builds anything for the user. It
  now reads: "Name any occasion, write your own words, and bring your imagination
  to life." The heading above it stays bold and unchanged.

Steps on the floating card:
- The five steps now sit on a single horizontal line at every width. Each step
  takes an equal share of the available space, with a smaller label, a smaller
  number badge and tighter padding, so Download no longer drops onto a second
  row.
- The sideways scrolling introduced in the previous version is no longer needed
  and has been removed. Measured against a 390 pixel phone, the widest step,
  Download, needs 62 pixels in the 67 pixels available.
- Step names remain visible at every screen size. They are never reduced to bare
  numbers.

Mobile floating card:
- The card and its step strip continue to float together as the page scrolls, on
  phones as well as desktop. The strip sits inside the floating card itself, so
  the current stage is always on screen with the card.

Cache:
- All asset version queries bumped to v28.
- cmm-build meta and service worker VERSION bumped to 28.

Verified before release:
- 17 live DOM and stylesheet checks covering the new wording, the single line
  step strip, and confirmation that the strip sits inside the floating column
  with sticky positioning preserved at every mobile breakpoint.

Unchanged:
- All ClaudeV27 functionality: 63 occasions, 7 categories, the removal of the
  duplicated how it works block, per line font and size controls, generated
  category and occasion pages, and the footer arrangement.
