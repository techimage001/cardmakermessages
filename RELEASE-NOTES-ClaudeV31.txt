Card Maker Messages ClaudeV31

Letter case control, proofreading and honest messaging.

New: letter case per line
- Every line on the card now has a third control beside font and size: As typed,
  UPPERCASE, lowercase or Title Case. That covers the occasion label, recipient
  dedication, front heading, front message, inside message, closing wish, sender
  and back message, on both flat and folded cards.
- This was built before the claim was made anywhere on the site. All eight
  controls were verified by rendering the card and confirming the pixels change.

Capitalisation across the site
- All 101 H1s and 781 statement headings now use Title Case, with articles,
  conjunctions and short prepositions correctly kept lowercase, so headings read
  as "Love and Relationships Card Messages" rather than "Love And Relationships".
- Question headings deliberately stay in sentence case, because that is how
  people phrase searches and how answer engines prefer to lift them. 122 question
  headings, 414 statement headings.
- Page titles and social titles were recapitalised to match.

Proofreading after the price word was removed
- 821 phrases were rewritten rather than patched. Constructions left behind by
  the earlier removal, including "available to use", "open, with nothing held
  back" and an orphaned fragment reading "What matters is this:", have been
  rewritten so they read as though the word was never there.
- Repeated phrasing was varied so the same substitute wording is not printed
  dozens of times across the site.

Watermark wording made specific
- The site now states "no watermarks on downloaded cards" rather than a loose
  "no watermark", which is precise and remains true if other things change.

Sign-up explained as a benefit
- Every page now carries wording explaining that after a few cards you will be
  invited to create an account, that signing in lets you return and carry on
  exactly where you left off, and that your cards and photographs stay on your
  own device rather than on our servers.
- The wording is varied per page so no two pages carry identical copy.

Integrity after all of the above
- 647 FAQ items, zero duplicate questions, zero duplicate answers.
- FAQ schema matches the visible text word for word on all 95 pages that have
  FAQs, rebuilt after every wording change.
- Zero broken internal links.
- Zero remaining instances of the price word, the false account claims, or
  grammar faults found by the scan.

Cache: all asset version queries, cmm-build meta and service worker at v31.
