Questions about new job card messages

How should I sign off a new job card?

Match the sign off to the message. A warm message closes naturally with something like "with love" and your name; a plainer message reads better with "best wishes" or simply your name on its own. On the card itself the closing wish and your name are separate lines, each with its own font and size control, so you can keep the closing line small and let it sit quietly under the message rather than competing with it. Avoid stacking several closings together. Nothing here is fixed: the new job wording, the design, the size and the font of each line can all be changed before you export the finished card.

Can I print a new job card at home?

Home printing works well if you get two settings right. Print at actual size rather than letting the printer scale the page, and when printing both sides choose landscape with short edge flipping so the inside lands the right way up. The exported PDF already has the fold line and panel positions worked out. Card stock behaves differently from paper in most home printers, so it is worth one test sheet before you commit the good stock. If you are still deciding, read the new job examples in two different tones and see which one you would be comfortable reading aloud to the person.

What is a short new job message?

Something like "Congratulations on the new job." does the job when space is tight or the relationship is more formal. Short messages are not a lesser option: on a new job card they often read as more sincere than a long one, because there is nowhere to hide behind phrasing. The rule that helps is to cut anything that would be true of anybody, and keep only what is true of this person. This page has several short options grouped together so you can compare them.

What do you write in a new job card?

Name one specific thing rather than reaching for a general sentiment. On a new job card that might be something the person did, something you noticed, or what you hope comes next for them. Two sincere sentences almost always read better than a paragraph of filler. There are 6 examples on this page across 4 tones, so you can find a line that matches your relationship and use it directly. If none fits exactly, take the closest one and change the details until it sounds like something you would actually say out loud.
