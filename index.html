Questions About Card Maker

Why am I asked to sign in after a few card cards?

You can start straight away, and after a few cards you will be asked to create an account. The reason is continuity: signing in lets you return to a card card you left half finished and pick it up on the same footing, instead of rebuilding it from scratch. What it does not do is move your work off your device. Cards and photographs stay where you made them, and the account simply recognises you when you come back.

Do I need to sign up before I start for index?

You can start straight away without signing up. The tool runs entirely inside your browser, so you can open it, build a card and download the result without setting anything up first. Your work is kept in the browser as you go, which means you can leave something half finished and return to it later on the same device. Any photograph you add stays on your device and is never uploaded to a server. The preview redraws as you work, so you are never guessing at what the exported file will look like.

Can I change the font and text size for index?

Yes. Every line has its own font and size control, so the occasion label, the heading, the main message, the closing wish and your name can each be set separately rather than following one template. There are seven typefaces and six sizes. All of the typefaces are standard system fonts, so there is nothing to download and no licensing restriction on anything you create. Changes appear on the live preview immediately. Everything described here applies to the card specifically, not just to the site in general.

Can I add my own photo with the index?

You can, and the photograph never leaves your device. That is a deliberate design choice rather than a limitation: photographs on cards are usually of family, and uploading them to a stranger's server to make a greeting card is an unnecessary risk. Position and zoom the image inside its frame, and replace or remove it at any point without losing your wording. The preview redraws as you work, so you are never guessing at what the exported file will look like. If something does not fit what you need, the wording, design, size and fonts can all be changed before export. This is the same across every index page on the site.

Can I print what I make for index?

Printing works well once two settings are right: print at actual size rather than letting the printer scale the page, and flip on the short edge when printing both sides. The exported PDF already has the fold line and panel positions worked out. Card stock feeds differently from paper in most home printers, so one test sheet before using the good stock is worth the minute it takes. If something does not fit what you need, the wording, design, size and fonts can all be changed before export. This is the same across every index page on the site.

What sizes can I export for index?

There are three families of size: digital, one page print and folded. Digital sizes are cut to the proportions the major messaging and social apps expect, which avoids the awkward cropping you get from a generic square. Print and folded sizes export at print resolution as a PDF. Switching between them mid way costs you nothing, since the wording and design carry across. The preview redraws as you work, so you are never guessing at what the exported file will look like. If something does not fit what you need, the wording, design, size and fonts can all be changed before export. This is the same across every index page on the site.

Does the card maker work on a phone for index?

Yes. The layout adapts to phone screens, with the card preview and the step navigation staying in view while you scroll through the controls beneath. You can write, design, preview and export entirely from a phone, including adding a photograph from your camera roll. Printing from a phone depends on your printer app, so for folded cards a laptop is usually easier at the final step. Everything described here applies to the card specifically, not just to the site in general. The preview redraws as you work, so you are never guessing at what the exported file will look like.

Will there be a watermark on my card for index?

No. Nothing you export carries a watermark, a logo or any reference to this site, at any size or in any format. That includes the PDF export, which on many comparable tools is where branding is added or resolution is reduced. What you download is the card exactly as you designed it, ready to print or send without anything to crop out first. If something does not fit what you need, the wording, design, size and fonts can all be changed before export.
