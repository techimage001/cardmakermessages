Questions About Thinking of You Card Messages

How long should a thinking of you message be?

One to three sentences suits most thinking of you cards. Shorter works better when the relationship is formal or the subject is delicate, because restraint reads as respect. Longer suits someone close who will read the card more than once. The format matters too: a folded card gives you an inside panel with room to write properly, while a card you send digitally reads better short enough to take in at a glance. When you are unsure, write the shorter version and let the specific detail carry the weight.

Can I add a photo to a thinking of you card?

Yes, and you can reposition and zoom it inside the frame until it sits how you want. The photograph is processed entirely in your browser and never reaches a server, so it stays private to you and whoever receives the card. You can remove or replace it at any point without losing your wording. If you are still deciding, read the thinking of you examples in two different tones and see which one you would be comfortable reading aloud to the person.

Can I print a thinking of you card at home?

Home printing works well if you get two settings right. Print at actual size rather than letting the printer scale the page, and when printing both sides choose landscape with short edge flipping so the inside lands the right way up. The exported PDF already has the fold line and panel positions worked out. Card stock behaves differently from paper in most home printers, so it is worth one test sheet before you commit the good stock. Because these thinking of you lines are written for this site rather than gathered from elsewhere, you can print any of them without worrying about who wrote them first.

What is a short thinking of you message?

Something like "Thinking of you." does the job when space is tight or the relationship is more formal. Short messages are not a lesser option: on a thinking of you card they often read as more sincere than a long one, because there is nowhere to hide behind phrasing. The rule that helps is to cut anything that would be true of anybody, and keep only what is true of this person. This page has several short options grouped together so you can compare them.

What do you write in a thinking of you card?

Be specific and be brief. The lines people remember on a thinking of you card are the ones that could only have been written about them. Avoid stock phrases that would suit anybody. This page groups 6 messages by tone so you can compare a warmer version against a plainer one before choosing. Whichever you pick, the card maker lets you edit every word afterwards, so nothing here locks you into wording that does not quite fit the person you are writing to.
