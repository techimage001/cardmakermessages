What can I write in a Valentine’s card?

Write in the voice you use with your partner. You can be tender, playful or deeply romantic, but include one detail that makes the message unmistakably theirs. Mention ordinary support, shared laughter or the feeling of being at home together.

  1. Choose romantic, heartfelt or playful wording.
  2. Name one quality or everyday action you love.
  3. Say what the relationship means to you.
  4. Close naturally with your name or personal sign-off.

What is a short valentine’s day message when making a valentines?

Something like "Happy Valentine’s Day to my favourite person." does the job when space is tight or the relationship is more formal. Short messages are not a lesser option: on a valentine’s day card they often read as more sincere than a long one, because there is nowhere to hide behind phrasing. How long should a valentine’s day message be on a valentines?

One to three sentences suits most valentine’s day cards. Shorter works better when the relationship is formal or the subject is delicate, because restraint reads as respect. Longer suits someone close who will read the card more than once. The format matters too: a folded card gives you an inside panel with room to write properly, while a card you send digitally reads better short enough to take in at a glance. When you are unsure, write the shorter version and let the specific detail carry the weight.
