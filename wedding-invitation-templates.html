What makes a wedding invitation template look elegant?

Elegant invitations use clear hierarchy, generous space and a limited palette. The couple’s names and date should stand out while venue and response information remain easy to find. Decorative elements should frame the wording rather than sit behind it or reduce contrast.

Can I print what I make when making a wedding invitation templates?
