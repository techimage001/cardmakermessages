Card Maker Messages ClaudeV23

Categories, per-line typography and the full occasion expansion.

Occasions and categories:
- Expanded from 22 to 63 occasions, with 246 new card messages written across
  heartfelt, short, funny, formal, religious, inspirational and romantic tones.
- Added 7 categories covering 60 occasion slots: Celebrate, Love and
  relationships, New beginnings, Support and care, Family and faith, Seasonal
  and cultural, Work and appreciation.
- New occasions include good luck, exam success, driving test, engagement,
  friendship, apology, new beginnings after divorce, pregnancy, baby shower,
  adoption, new home, housewarming, new job, starting university, welcome back,
  thinking of you, sympathy, pet loss, pregnancy loss, serious illness,
  encouragement, difficult times, baptism, christening, First Holy Communion,
  confirmation, pastor appreciation, church anniversary, New Year, Eid al-Fitr,
  Eid al-Adha, Ramadan, Diwali, Hanukkah, Lunar New Year, Vaisakhi, farewell,
  teacher, colleague, boss and volunteer appreciation.
- Category and occasion dropdowns added to the Message step. Pick a category and
  the occasion list filters to it. The live card updates immediately on both.
- Every one of the 63 occasions was rendered and checked during the build.

Text style for each line:
- Each line on the card now has its own font and size control, laid out beside
  the line name: occasion label, recipient dedication, front heading, front
  message, inside message, closing wish, sender and back message.
- Seven font choices, all standard system font stacks with no licensing or
  copyright risk: design default, serif, sans, classic, modern, typewriter and
  handwritten. Six sizes from small to largest, clamped so extreme values cannot
  break the layout.
- The closing wish and the sender are now drawn as separate lines rather than one
  block, so each can be styled on its own.
- All twelve controls were verified by rendering the card before and after each
  change and confirming the output actually differs.

Search and internal linking:
- 51 new pages generated: 7 category pages and 44 occasion message pages, each
  with its own title, description, canonical, Open Graph tags and BreadcrumbList
  structured data.
- Every occasion page links to its category, to sibling occasions in that
  category and into the card maker with the occasion preselected. Every category
  page links to all of its occasions and across to the other categories.
- The 7 categories now appear in the footer of all 101 pages.
- Sitemap regenerated with 100 URLs.
- All 7429 internal page links were checked. None are broken.

Workflow:
- The step strip on the floating live card now shows the step names, not just
  numbers, so the current stage is always readable while the card stays in view.
  Names collapse to numbers on very narrow screens.

Cache:
- All asset version queries bumped to v23.
- cmm-build meta and service worker VERSION bumped to 23.

Unchanged:
- All ClaudeV22 functionality: recipient dedication on single cards, sender
  sizing, motif clearance, aspect-aware photo frames, editable occasion label,
  clean folded backs, the pinned guard reminder, review share and save options,
  folded exports, email verification, SMTP, admin dashboard, and the c.php and
  og.php share previews.
- Sharing is unchanged: cards without a photo share as a link with a preview;
  cards with a photo share as an image.
